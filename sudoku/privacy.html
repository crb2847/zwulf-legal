Privacy Policy — Sudoku & Dragons

Android package com.zwulf.sudoku · Published by Zwulf · Effective September 23, 2026

Summary

Sudoku & Dragons does not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no advertising, no analytics, and no third-party tracking.

Information we collect

None. The app does not ask for your name, email address, location, contacts, device identifiers, or any other personal data, and it does not send any information from your device to us or to anyone else.

Data stored on your device

Sudoku & Dragons saves your in-app progress and statistics (for example games played, completion times, the current puzzle, and your dragon's progress) locally on your device so they are there the next time you open the app. This data never leaves your device. It is deleted when you clear the app's data or uninstall the app.

Sharing

If you tap Share on a result card, Sudoku & Dragons creates an image of that card on your device and hands it to Android's share sheet. Where it goes next is up to the app you pick. We never receive it.

Permissions

The app declares the Android INTERNET permission because the framework it is built with (Capacitor) requires it to render the app. Sudoku & Dragons does not use it to transmit any personal data.

Third-party services

The app contains no third-party SDKs for advertising, analytics, crash reporting, or social features. It is distributed through Google Play, whose own privacy policy governs the Play Store itself.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, of any age.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever begins collecting data, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the effective date above will change.

Contact

Questions about this policy: developer@zwulf.com
